# EchoSign Attest — Landing & Docs Outline (E9-1)

## Positioning

> Cryptographic proof that an audio recording existed, unaltered, under a controlled key, at a specific time — verifiable by anyone offline.

Not "AI detector". Not "courtroom expertise". Integrity + provenance + chain-of-custody.

## Target audiences

1. Regulated contact centres
2. Notaries / legal tech
3. Media / journalism

## Honesty banner (required on every page)

> This product proves cryptographic integrity and time of existence.
> It does **not** prove that a human was speaking or that the content is true.
