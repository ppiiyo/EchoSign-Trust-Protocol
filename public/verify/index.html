EchoSign Offline Verifier

Runs entirely in your browser via Web Crypto. Nothing is uploaded.

Honest scope. A valid signature proves integrity of the signed manifest (and the audio contentHash it binds) since signing time. It does not prove a human was speaking, detect deepfakes, or constitute a court seal.
Proves: integrity of signed bytes at signing time.
Does not prove: human speaker · deepfake absence · legal certification.
Ed25519 via Web Crypto. Full TSA/OTS: node scripts/verify-bundle.mjs
